I do not share it with lead generators or unrelated advertisers. I may use vendors that only send messages for me (for example the texting provider). Those vendors are not given your number to market their own products.

Texts, HELP, and STOP

Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help. You can also call (614) 980-9166. After STOP I will not text that number for this program unless you opt in again.

How long I keep it

I keep your number and opt-in record while you are opted in, and as long as I need a record of consent or opt-out. Opt-out requests are honored promptly.
