Privacy policy

Effective August 17, 2026. Last updated August 17, 2026.

This policy describes how Christopher Vaughn, a sole proprietor doing business as Christopher Vaughn Insurance in Ohio (“I,” “me”), handles information for this SMS program. It is written so mobile carriers can review this messaging program. It is not legal advice.

Who this covers

People who request an insurance quote or discuss an existing policy with me and who opt in to text messages from Christopher Vaughn Insurance.

Information I collect

  • Name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Quote or policy details you already shared so I can follow up on that business
  • SMS opt-in, opt-out, and help requests

How I use it

I use this information to send SMS about your own insurance quote or policy: appointment reminders, follow-ups, and answers to questions. Message frequency varies. You may receive up to 8 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply.

Mobile information is not sold or shared for marketing

No mobile data will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes at any time. I do not sell your mobile number. I do not share it with lead generators or unrelated advertisers. I may use vendors that only process messages for me (for example the texting provider that delivers the SMS). Those vendors are not given your number to market their own products.

Texts, HELP, and STOP

Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help. You can also call (614) 980-9166. After STOP I will not text that number for this program unless you opt in again.

How long I keep it

I keep your number and opt-in record while you are opted in and as long as I need a record of consent or opt-out for this program and insurance follow-up. Opt-out requests are honored promptly.
