About

Christopher Vaughn is a licensed insurance agent based in Columbus, Ohio. He is a sole proprietor doing business as Christopher Vaughn Insurance. This website is his public page: who he is, where he is licensed, and how to opt in to text updates.

He is an independent contractor appointed with American Income Life Insurance Company to offer life and supplemental insurance products. He is not an officer or employee of American Income Life, Globe Life Inc., or any field agency, and he does not have general authority to transact business for those companies.

Who I work with

People assigned to me for a new insurance quote, people who already have a policy I service, and people they refer to me. I text only after that person opts in. This is not a purchased list and not third-party lead generation.

Where I am licensed

I solicit and service insurance only in states where I hold an active license:
